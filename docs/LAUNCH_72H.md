# Sim Venture — 72 Hour Launch Scope

## Launch promise
Test your startup before you build it.

## Non-negotiable user paths
1. Search / Explore real app ideas and competitors.
2. Create a venture from a rough idea.
3. Venture Home with clear next action.
4. Research with source-status truth labels.
5. Shape the idea into user/problem/value/MVP.
6. Monetization hypotheses and tests.
7. Compare two ventures side by side.
8. Simulator with decisions, time, consequences, rewinds and Reality Mode.
9. Build Studio with version-one scope and dependencies.
10. Learn with simulation vs real outcomes.
11. Investor World with adaptive rehearsal, claim ledger, diligence, committee and deal mechanics.
12. Simple Mode default; Pro Mode for evidence/technology/deeper detail.
13. Reliable authentication and production deployment.
14. Mobile and visual-hierarchy pass.

## Source truth state at launch
- Apple iTunes Search API — LIVE — competitor/app discovery and rating-count signals only.
- World Bank Open Data — LIVE — country-level indicators only; not direct TAM.
- GitHub repository search — PARTIAL — ecosystem/technology signal only.
- App review intelligence — UNAVAILABLE until reliable provider is connected.
- Competitor pricing intelligence — UNAVAILABLE until reliable provider is connected.
- Regulatory intelligence — UNAVAILABLE until jurisdiction-specific authoritative sources are connected.
- Google Play discovery/reviews — NOT CONNECTED for launch unless a reliable permitted provider is added.

## Truth rules
Never fabricate downloads, revenue, market share, traction, willingness to pay, pricing, investor interest or success probability. Keep source facts, findings, assumptions, founder claims, simulated outcomes and real outcomes visibly separate.

## 72-hour execution order
### Day 1 — Core functional completeness
- Real Search / Explore results dashboard using live connected sources.
- Search → create/open venture flow.
- Ensure Compare 2 Ventures is discoverable and usable.
- Audit every primary route for dead ends, placeholder/demo leakage and missing source-status labels.
- Fix authentication reliability enough for external testers.

### Day 2 — Product quality
- Visual hierarchy and distinct module identity.
- Simulator causality/decision UX and branch comparison polish.
- Investor World usability and voice fallback.
- Mobile layout and navigation.
- Landing/onboarding/pricing/access copy.

### Day 3 — Launch hardening
- Full end-to-end test: signup → idea → research → shape → monetize → compare → simulate → build → investor → learn.
- CI, typecheck, lint, tests, production build.
- Production database/RLS/security smoke test.
- Final domain/email setup if approved.
- Analytics/error monitoring if available.
- Launch checklist and public link.

## Deferred until after launch
Community/public venture mechanics, advanced billing tiers, universities/incubators, large paid datasets, full avatar video investor, advanced Google Play intelligence, portfolio/community mechanics.
